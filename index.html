Services & Treatment Conditions

Explore our range of clinical therapies, functional assessments, and specialized rehabilitation care.

Our Comprehensive Services

Functional Assessment

In-depth evaluations using evidence-based tools to identify strengths, challenges, and tailored strategies for support.

Early Identification & Intervention

Collaborating with Community Health Volunteers (CHVs) for early screening, assessment, and referrals.

Rehabilitation & Therapies

Comprehensive paediatric and adult therapies including Physiotherapy, Speech Therapy, and Occupational Therapy.

Sensory Integration & CBT

Helping individuals regulate sensory input and develop emotional coping mechanisms via Cognitive Behavioral Therapy.

Mental Health Services

Dedicated support for mothers and caregivers experiencing depression, stress, and burnout.

Education Support

Tailored school-based and home-based education support for students with Special Educational Needs (SEN).

Conditions We Treat

  • Spina Bifida

Core Strategic Programmes

Discover our four key community initiatives designed for sustainable impact.

Programme 01

Early Intervention is the Best ("Mapema Ndio Best")

Tackles delayed identification and referral of children with disabilities by offering timely screening, comprehensive functional assessment, and early therapeutic interventions.

Focus: Early Screening & Assessment
Programme 02

Adopt a School, Enrol a Child

Confronts the reluctance of regular schools to admit children with disabilities by placing assessed children into adopted mainstream schools with tailored IEP/SEN support.

Focus: Inclusive Education Placement
Programme 03

Health for Quality Education

Addresses health-related absenteeism and dropouts by promptly diagnosing and managing health challenges to ensure student retention and academic progression.

Focus: School Health & Retention
Programme 04

Universal Empowerment for Success

Empowers families through structured initiatives like "My Mummy and I" and "Siblings and I" to provide peer support, counseling, and practical caregiving skills.

Focus: Family Support & Caregivers
